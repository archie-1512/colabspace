# TripTrail v1.0 Launch Plan

Owner: Maya Chen (Head of Product)
Target public launch: Saturday 31 October 2026

## Launch phases
1. **Closed beta (done).** 120 testers from the waitlist. Feedback triaged into the Product Launch board.
2. **Soft launch, 24 October.** Android only, Kerala and Tamil Nadu users, no paid marketing.
3. **Public launch, 31 October.** Android + iOS, all of India, launch blog post and Instagram campaign go live the same morning.

## Go / no-go criteria
- Payments working in live mode (Razorpay). This is currently the biggest risk: live keys are blocked on KYC approval.
- Crash-free sessions above 99.5% on Android for 3 consecutive days.
- p95 latency on /search under 800 ms at 500 requests per second.
- App Store approval for the iOS build.

If payments are not live by 27 October, we launch with "Pay at pickup" only and switch online payments on later. This fallback was agreed in the 2 October leadership meeting.
